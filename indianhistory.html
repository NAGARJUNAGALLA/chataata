Major Indian Kingdoms & Empires

A chronological overview from Ancient Mahajanapadas to the Modern Era

c. 600 BCE – 345 BCE

1. Sixteen Mahajanapadas & Nanda Empire

Region: Indo-Gangetic Plain | Key Rulers: Bimbisara, Ajatashatru, Mahapadma Nanda

The rise of urban states across northern India, culminating in Magadha's dominance under the Haryanka, Shishunaga, and Nanda dynasties.

322 BCE – 185 BCE

2. Maurya Empire

Region: Pan-Indian Subcontinent | Key Rulers: Chandragupta Maurya, Bindusara, Ashoka

First empire to unify most of the Indian subcontinent, renowned for Arthashastra administration and Ashoka's spread of Buddhism.

c. 300 BCE – 1279 CE

3. Sangam Dynasties (Chera, Chola, Pandya)

Region: Southern India (Tamilakam) | Key Rulers: Karikala Chola, Nedunjeliyan, Rajaraja I, Rajendra I

Three ancient Tamil dynasties flourishing from the Sangam period, later evolving into medieval maritime superpowers under the Imperial Cholas.

c. 1st Century BCE – 220 CE

4. Satavahana Dynasty

Region: Deccan Plateau | Key Rulers: Gautamiputra Satakarni, Vashishthiputra Pulumavi

Dominant Deccan power acting as a cultural and trade bridge between North and South India, noted for Amaravati art and maritime commerce.

c. 30 CE – 375 CE

5. Kushan Empire

Region: Northern & Northwestern India | Key Rulers: Kujula Kadphises, Kanishka the Great

Controlled key Silk Road routes, fostering Gandhara and Mathura schools of art and the spread of Mahayana Buddhism into Central Asia.

c. 319 CE – 550 CE

6. Gupta Empire

Region: Northern & Central India | Key Rulers: Chandragupta I, Samudragupta, Chandragupta II

Widely regarded as the Golden Age of India for breakthroughs in mathematics, astronomy, Sanskrit literature, and temple architecture.

543 CE – 753 CE & 973 CE – 1189 CE

7. Chalukya Dynasties (Badami & Kalyani)

Region: Karnataka & Deccan | Key Rulers: Pulakeshin II, Vikramaditya VI

Pioneers of Vesara temple architecture at Badami, Aihole, and Pattadakal, successfully checking northern expansion into the Deccan.

275 CE – 897 CE

8. Pallava Dynasty

Region: Northern Tamil Nadu & Southern Andhra | Key Rulers: Mahendravarman I, Narasimhavarman I

Renowned patrons of Dravidian rock-cut and structural architecture, exemplified by the shore temples and monuments of Mahabalipuram.

750 CE – 1161 CE

9. Pala Empire

Region: Bengal & Bihar | Key Rulers: Gopala, Dharmapala, Devapala

Major patrons of Mahayana and Vajrayana Buddhism, establishing renowned monastic universities at Vikramashila and supporting Nalanda.

753 CE – 982 CE

10. Rashtrakuta Dynasty

Region: Deccan & Central India | Key Rulers: Dantidurga, Dhruva, Amoghavarsha I

Dominated the Tripartite Struggle for Kannauj and commissioned the monolithic Kailasa Temple at Ellora.

1206 CE – 1526 CE

11. Delhi Sultanate

Region: Northern & Central India | Dynasties: Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi

Introduced Indo-Islamic architecture and centralized military-administrative systems while defending the subcontinent against Mongol invasions.

1336 CE – 1646 CE

12. Vijayanagara Empire

Region: Southern India | Key Rulers: Harihara I, Bukka Raya I, Krishnadevaraya

Centered at Hampi, the empire served as a wealthy bastion of South Indian arts, Telugu/Kannada/Tamil literature, and temple building.

1526 CE – 1857 CE

13. Mughal Empire

Region: Almost Entire Indian Subcontinent | Key Rulers: Babur, Akbar, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb

Unified vast territories with centralized revenue systems and left an enduring architectural legacy including the Taj Mahal and Red Fort.

1674 CE – 1818 CE

14. Maratha Empire

Region: Deccan, Central & Northern India | Key Rulers: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, Peshwa Bajirao I

Rose from the Western Ghats using agile guerrilla warfare and naval power to become the dominant Indian confederacy in the 18th century.

1799 CE – 1849 CE

15. Sikh Empire

Region: Punjab & Northwestern Subcontinent | Key Rulers: Maharaja Ranjit Singh

Consolidated the Sikh Misls into a powerful secular kingdom stretching from the Sutlej River to the Khyber Pass.
